A History of Tokyo

This is a private study site about the history of Tokyo. It begins with the land, and with the people who lived here before 1590. Then it follows the city as it grew from a castle town into one of the largest cities on earth. The walking pages are for use on foot in the city. They show the same streets in five periods, from Edo to today. Each map names its sources on the page itself.
