Read me first

Each coloured shape is a ring. It shows how far the city reached in one year. The oldest rings are the darkest. Move the slider, or press Play, to watch the city spread. Tap a ring to see how it was made.

The rings do not all measure the same thing. Most show built-up land: streets and buildings. Some show a legal city line, which can take in fields and woods. The 1818 red line is a legal line. It is much larger than the built-up city of its day. The 1878 ring, the fifteen wards, is also a legal line. The 1945 ring comes from survey sheets only partly brought up to date in 1944–47. So it does not show the fire damage of 1945.

The 1457 and 1632 rings are sketches. The 1457 ring was drawn from written accounts, not from a map of the time. The 1632 ring was traced from one old map, and its edges may be hundreds of metres out. Treat both as rough pictures.
